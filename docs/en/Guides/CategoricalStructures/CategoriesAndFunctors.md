## Abstract

Category theory reads a rewriting system as objects and the morphisms between
them, and a rewrite step as a universal construction: categories and the quivers
that present them; functors and natural transformations; limits, colimits,
products, pullbacks and pushouts; the category of hypergraph states and their
morphisms; double pushout rewriting, where a rule is a span of monomorphisms and
an event is a pair of pushout squares; and the reading of a multiway system as a
category, its tokens and events forming one graph and its branches the homotopies
between paths.
