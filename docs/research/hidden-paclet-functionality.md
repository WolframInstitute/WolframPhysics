# Hidden and experimental paclet functionality

Census of what the physics paclets of the ecosystem export beyond their documented surface: the symbols a paclet's PacletInfo does not declare, its experimental and optimization subcontexts, its PackageScope, the properties its objects answer, and the exports of the GitHub-only Wolfram Institute paclets. Every symbol here was seen by Names in a WL 15.0 kernel or in a PackageExport line of a Kernel file (installed or fetched raw from GitHub), and every URL was fetched on 2026-09-21. Date: 2026-09-21.

## Summary of what exists

- Wolfram/QuantumFramework 2.1.1 declares 26 symbols, its main context loads 50, its Experimental context 7, its QuantumOptimization context 15, its Gates context 28 and its PackageScope 228. The physics-relevant hidden surface is the multiway family of Kernel/Multiway.m (QuantumCircuitMultiwayGraph, QuantumCircuitMultiwayCausalGraph, QuantumCircuitPathGraph, QuantumCircuitTokenEventGraph), the ZX family of Kernel/ZX.m, the tensor network family of Kernel/QuantumCircuitOperator/TensorNetwork.m (TensorNetworkQuantumCircuit exported; QuantumCircuitHypergraph, QuantumTensorNetworkGraph, QuantumTensorNetwork and TensorNetworkCompile in PackageScope, reached through the circuit properties `"Hypergraph"`, `"TensorNetworkGraph"` and `"TensorNetwork"`), the Experimental path-sum and pruning functions FeynmanBacktracking and QuantumBeamSearch, the matrix product functions QuantumMPS, QuantumMPO and QuantumMPSApply, the phase space inverses QuantumWeylTransform and QuantumPositiveTransform, and the stabilizer objects StabilizerFrame and LocalComplement. The guides already list QuantumCircuitMultiwayGraph, ZXTensorNetwork, ZXExpression, ZXTensorNetworkQuantumCircuit and QuantumMPS, and carry the other three multiway graphs only in the BranchialGraphs tail; 25 rows below add or cross-list the rest. The DiagramPlot context (QuantumCircuitDiagram, DiagramPlot) is declared but never loaded in 2.1.1.
- Wolfram/Multicomputation 0.1.9 declares 5 symbols and loads 41 plus 22 PackageScope names. The guides already list 24 of the 41; the 15 rows below add the rule application functions (ApplyHypergraphRules, ApplyWolframModelRules, ApplyCARules), the rule and tree conversions of the linked hypergraph encoding, the graph helpers and the two vertex shape functions. A Multi answers 59 property strings and a MultiwaySystem 24, among them the Function Repository compatible `"CausalInvariantQ"`, `"TotalCausalInvariantQ"`, `"CanonicalKnuthBendixCompletion"`, `"BranchPairResolutionsList"` and `"AllStatesBranchialGraph"`, which no research report had recorded.
- SetReplace 0.3.196 loads 57 symbols, all documented on GitHub; the guides list 43 of them. The eight rows below are the type system and generator enumeration objects (SetReplaceType, SetReplaceProperty, SetReplaceMethodImplementation and the `$SetReplaceSystems`, `$SetReplaceGenerators`, `$SetReplaceTypes`, `$SetReplaceTypeGraph` lists) and `$WolframModelRuleProperties`.
- WolframInstitute/Hypergraph 1.2.3 declares 58 and loads 59; the one undeclared symbol CanonicalHypergraphGraded is already listed, and KoszulSign, declared and documented, is the one row.
- Wolfram/Lambda 1.2.4 declares 57 and loads 91; the guides list 71. The 14 rows are the single-way causal graph and path event functions, the term utilities and the two data stores.
- Wolfram/Metamathematics 1.0.2 (sw1sh/WolframMetamathematics on GitHub) declares nothing and loads 52; the guides list 17. The 10 rows are the quantifier elimination functions of Kernel/Quantifiers.m, the disagreement set of Robinson unification, and the positioned match and replace primitives.
- WolframInstitute/HypergraphRewriteEngine, WolframInstitute/TuringMachine and WolframInstitute/CellularAutomaton have no unlisted physics-relevant symbol: the seven HGSession symbols and every HG* symbol of the installed 0.0.1, all 30 loaded TuringMachine symbols except three aliases, and all 12 CellularAutomaton symbols except one constraint constant are on the guides.
- Wolfram/DiagrammaticComputation 1.0.8 declares 95, all loaded, plus 36 undeclared subcontext names. The 12 rows are the three interaction net rule sets for lambda reduction, GridFoliation, and eight declared symbols with reference notebooks that no guide lists.
- WolframInstitute/Infrageometry on GitHub main is 1.0.59 against the installed 1.0.14; the guides already carry 65 of the 66 GitHub-only exports. The 30 rows are the simplicial complex functions of Kernel/Experimental.wl, the hypergraph and network datasets, the mesh complexes and the graded matrix functions. Eleven installed-only names (WolframRicciCurvature and relatives) were renamed on GitHub and are recorded as superseded.
- WolframInstitute/InfraCausality 1.2.0 and WolframInstitute/InfraGaugeTheory 1.0.7 are fully listed except five worldline helpers of InfraCausality. WolframInstitute/SyntheticInfrageometry on GitHub main is 0.13.31 against the installed 0.12.36 and exports 187 symbols of which the guides list 96; the 75 rows are its constructions, predicates and axiom tests, with the 14 color and size settings recorded as omitted.
- Nothing was installed for this census: every paclet read from the kernel was already present under the user's paclet repository (versions above), and InfraCausality, InfraGaugeTheory, SyntheticInfrageometry main and the newer Infrageometry were read from GitHub raw files. The kernel is not cloud connected; ResourceObject was used only to test public Function Repository names.
